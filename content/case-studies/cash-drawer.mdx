<CaseSection id="customer">

The tasting room staff at 14 Acres Vineyard & Winery — **8–10 people** — who count the cash drawer, and the managers who rely on those counts. Cash sales are rung up in the **Clover POS**.

<CaseSection id="discovery">

**What they had:** paper cash counts that weren’t accurate, and a buggy prototype meant to replace them.

**The real problem:** a staff member had built the first version with AI. It was constantly buggy, and the staff who used it complained about how it worked. So I asked each of them what their biggest pain points were, and rebuilt the app in Next.js around their answers.

</CaseSection>

<CaseSection id="constraints">

- **Existing systems:** the source of truth for cash sales is Clover, so every count is checked against Clover cash sales.
- **Users:** 8–10 staff use it, so it has to be quick to learn and quick to use during a shift.
- **The first version:** constant bugs and an interface staff complained about, so the rebuild had to be reliable and work the way each of them needed.
